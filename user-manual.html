ChurchTrack User Manual

1. Introduction

ChurchTrack is a web-based church membership and attendance management system developed for Christ-Like Christian Church (CLCC). It enables church leadership to track member attendance, manage church events, maintain membership records, and facilitate family connections among members.

The system supports three user roles:

  • Member â€” Church members who can view their attendance history, access their personal QR code for check-ins, and manage their family connections.
  • Manager â€” Church staff responsible for scanning member QR codes to record attendance, managing events, and monitoring member records.
  • Administrator â€” Church administrators with full access to all system features including reports, settings, and member approval.

2. System Requirements

ChurchTrack is a web-based application accessible through any modern web browser. No software installation is required.

Recommended Browsers

  • Google Chrome (version 90 or higher)
  • Mozilla Firefox (version 88 or higher)
  • Microsoft Edge (version 90 or higher)
  • Safari (version 14 or higher)

Device Requirements

  • Desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone with internet access
  • Stable internet connection
  • Camera access required for QR code scanning (Manager portal only)

4. Member User Guide

4.1 Registration

New members may create an account through the registration page.

  1. From the homepage, click Sign Up or Register.
  2. Fill in all required fields: First Name, Last Name, Username, Email Address, Birthday, Password, and Confirm Password.
  3. Accept the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
  4. Click Register. A verification email will be sent.
Note: Members under 18 require parent or guardian approval before the account becomes fully active.

4.2 Email Verification

  1. Open your email and find the message from ChurchTrack with subject "Verify Your Email."
  2. Click the verification link. You will be redirected to a confirmation page.
  3. Your account will then be reviewed and approved by the church administrator.
Note: If no email was received, check your spam or junk mail folder.

4.3 Login

  1. Navigate to the Login page.
  2. Enter your Username and Password.
  3. Click Sign In.

  4. Enter and confirm your new password, then click Reset Password.

  4. The member's name and check-in status appear instantly.

7. Troubleshooting

Cannot Log In

  • Check username and password. Verify Caps Lock is off.
  • Use Forgot Password on the login page to reset.
  • If not yet verified or approved, contact your administrator.

Did Not Receive Verification Email

  • Check your spam or junk mail folder.
  • Ensure the email address entered during registration was correct.
  • If the problem persists, contact your church administrator.

QR Code Not Showing

  • Check internet connection and refresh the page.
  • Log out and log back in if the issue persists.

QR Code Cannot Be Scanned

  • Tap Fullscreen to maximize the QR code display.
  • Increase screen brightness. Hold the device steady.

Attendance Not Recorded After Scanning

  • Confirm the correct event is selected in Scan Attendance.
  • Confirm the event status is Active. Completed events do not accept new check-ins.
  • Check internet connection and scan again.

Camera Not Working

  • Ensure the browser has camera permission enabled.
  • On mobile: open browser settings â†’ enable camera access for ChurchTrack.
  • Try using Google Chrome.

Page Not Loading / Slow Performance

  • Check internet connection.
  • Clear browser cache and cookies, then reload.
  • Try a different browser or device.
